


# Features

1. The goal of the game is to stay undistracted for as long as you can
    - without stopping, i.e., dropping your speed below 10 miles per hour
    - without turning into a side road and leave the page through a portal
    - if you just put on autodrive, you can certainly drive for a long time but can you resist the temptation of all the billboards?
2. Drive distance measures driving continuously
    - stopping the car or getting back from a portal will set this back to 0
